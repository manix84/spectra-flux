# Privacy Policy 🔒

SpectraFlux is a browser-only audio visualizer. It is designed to process audio locally in your browser so the visuals can react in real time.

## What SpectraFlux Handles 🎧

- 🎵 Audio files you choose in the app
- 🎙️ Microphone input you explicitly allow in the browser
- 🖥️ Tab, screen, or system audio you explicitly share through browser capture prompts
- ⚙️ Browser-side app state needed for the visualizer experience

## What Stays Local 🏠

Audio analysis happens in the browser using Web Audio APIs. The app does not upload your audio, recordings, microphone input, screen content, or visualizer settings to a project server.

## Browser Permissions 🛂

Your browser controls microphone and display capture permissions. You can revoke those permissions in your browser or operating system settings at any time.

## Local Storage and Offline Use 📦

SpectraFlux includes PWA assets and a service worker so the app shell can work offline after it has been loaded. Cached app files may be stored by your browser. Clearing browser site data removes those cached files.

## Third Parties 🌐

The app does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party telemetry. Browser, hosting, or network providers may still process standard request metadata when you load the site.

## Contact 📬

Open an issue or use the project support channel if you notice behaviour that does not match this policy.
