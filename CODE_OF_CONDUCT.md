# Code of Conduct 🌈

This project follows a simple rule: be respectful, constructive, and practical.

## Expected Behaviour ✅

- Treat contributors with patience and professionalism.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about technical concerns.

## Unacceptable Behaviour 🚫

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated bad-faith disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that does not fit these expectations.
